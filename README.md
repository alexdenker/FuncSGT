# Supervised Guidance Training for Infinite-Dimensional Diffusion Models

Official implementation of the paper  
📄 *Supervised Guidance Training for Infinite-Dimensional Diffusion Models*  
by [Elizabeth L. Baker](https://libbylbaker.github.io/), [Alexander Denker](https://alexdenker.github.io/), and [Jes Frellsen](https://frellsen.org/).

> Score-based diffusion models have recently been extended to infinite-dimensional function spaces, enabling applications such as Bayesian inverse problems governed by PDEs.
>
> This work introduces **Supervised Guidance Training (SGT)**, a simulation-free score matching objective for efficiently conditioning diffusion models in function space. We derive an infinite-dimensional extension of Doob’s \(h\)-transform and show that the conditional score decomposes into an unconditional score and a guidance term.
>
> The proposed method enables stable and accurate posterior sampling for infinite-dimensional diffusion models.
